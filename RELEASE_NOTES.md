YTXTube Custom 2.6.25: videos keep playing when YouTube refuses a stream, flex mode fix

- Videos no longer stop about a minute in with "YouTube refused the stream (HTTP 403 · IOS)": the app switches to another stream right away, sometimes at a lower quality
- After turning the screen off and on with a video open, flex mode no longer shows when the phone is flat open, or stays away when it is half open
- Settings, Flex mode now also shows what the phone's fold report says, to help track down any flex mode mix-up
